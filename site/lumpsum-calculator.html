Lumpsum Calculator (Educational)

Invest once and let it grow. See how a one-time amount compounds over the years.

Formula

Future Value = P × (1 + r)ⁿ

P = amount · r = yearly rate · n = years

Explained with real-life examples

₹1 lakh left alone for 10 years

Karthik invests a ₹1,00,000 bonus once and doesn't touch it for 10 years, assuming 12% a year.

Result: It could grow to about ₹3,10,585, more than three times the original amount.

A safer 5-year plan

Meena puts ₹5,00,000 into a lower-risk option for 5 years at 7% a year.

Result: It could grow to about ₹7,01,276. Lower risk usually means lower growth.

Returns and rates here are assumptions to show how the maths works. Nothing is guaranteed.
