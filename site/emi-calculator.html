Loan EMI Calculator (Educational)

See your monthly EMI and how much interest a loan really costs.

Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

P = loan · r = monthly rate · n = months

Explained with real-life examples

A ₹30 lakh home loan

Ravi takes a ₹30,00,000 home loan for 20 years at 8.5% a year.

Result: His EMI is about ₹26,035 a month, and he pays about ₹32,48,327 in interest, more than the loan itself.

A ₹8 lakh car loan

Divya borrows ₹8,00,000 for 5 years at 9% a year.

Result: Her EMI is about ₹16,607 and total interest is about ₹1,96,401. Shorter loans cost far less interest.

Returns and rates here are assumptions to show how the maths works. Nothing is guaranteed.
