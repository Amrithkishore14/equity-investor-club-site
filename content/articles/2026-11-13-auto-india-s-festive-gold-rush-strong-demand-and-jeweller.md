## What happened

Indian consumers have shown a remarkable increase in gold purchases, with demand rising by approximately 30% in volume terms during the recent festive period, including Navaratri and Diwali. This surge is largely attributed to a correction in gold prices, which made the precious metal more accessible to buyers. The cultural significance of buying gold during festivals and the ongoing wedding season further fueled this demand across the country.

Major jewellery retailers have benefited from this heightened consumer interest. Kalyan Jewellers, for instance, reported strong financial results for the second quarter of the financial year 2027 (Q2 FY27), with its consolidated net profit jumping by 63.60% year-on-year to ₹427 crore. The company's revenue also saw a significant increase of 31.4% to ₹10,323 crore in the September quarter, as reported by Mint.

Looking ahead, the outlook for the current festive quarter (Q3 FY27) remains optimistic for leading jewellery companies. Titan Company, another prominent player, also reported a substantial 54% rise in consolidated net profit for Q2 FY27, driven by strong demand across its jewellery, watches, and eyewear segments. While Thangamayil Jewellery's Q2 FY27 net profit remained largely flat despite a 41% revenue surge, the festive season is expected to provide a strong boost. Industry leaders, including Kalyan Jewellers' Executive Director Ramesh Kalyanaraman, have expressed strong encouragement regarding the growth observed during Navaratri and Dhanteras, anticipating a robust end to the wedding season.

## What it means for you

For beginner investors, this trend highlights how consumer sentiment, cultural events, and price movements can significantly impact specific sectors. The jewellery industry in India is deeply intertwined with festivals and wedding seasons, making it a cyclical sector. A price correction in gold can stimulate demand, leading to increased sales and potentially better financial performance for companies in this space. Understanding these dynamics can help investors identify potential opportunities and risks related to consumer discretionary spending and commodity price fluctuations.

## Key takeaways

*   Indian gold demand increased by about 30% in volume during the recent festive season due to price corrections.
*   Festivals like Navaratri and Diwali, along with the wedding season, are key drivers for gold sales.
*   Kalyan Jewellers reported strong Q2 FY27 results, with significant profit and revenue growth.
*   Other major jewellers like Titan and Thangamayil are also expected to see positive results, especially in the festive quarter.

## Jargon buster

*   **Price Correction:** A significant drop in the price of an asset (like gold) after a period of sustained increase, often seen as a return to a more realistic value.
*   **Quarterly Results:** Financial reports released by public companies every three months, detailing their performance, including revenue, profit, and expenses, for that period.
